# Developer Reward Tiers

## How Tiers Work

Every approved app moves you up toward the next tier. Tiers unlock **free Agent Midas subscriptions** that stack on top of your cash bounties and affiliate commissions.

Tiers are **permanent** -- once you reach a tier, you keep it.

---

|------|---------------|-------------------|---------------|--------------|

## What Each Subscription Includes

### Starter ($29/mo)
- 1 AI Agent (MIDAS)
- Basic CRM (500 contacts)
- Email campaigns (1,000/mo)
- Basic analytics

### Professional ($79/mo)
- 3 AI Agents
- Full CRM (5,000 contacts)
- Email campaigns (10,000/mo)
- Advanced analytics + A/B testing
- Social media scheduler

### Business ($199/mo)
- 5 AI Agents
- Full CRM (25,000 contacts)
- Email campaigns (50,000/mo)
- All analytics features
- Help desk + live chat
- Content management

### Premium ($399/mo)
- 10 AI Agents
- Unlimited contacts
- Unlimited email campaigns
- All features
- Priority support
- Custom integrations

### Enterprise ($599/mo)
- Unlimited AI Agents
- Unlimited everything
- White-label option
- Dedicated support
- Custom development
- SLA guarantee
